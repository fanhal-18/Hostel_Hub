Features

Everything You Need, All in One Place

HostelHub gives students and administrators a powerful, intuitive platform to manage every aspect of hostel life.

🏠

Room Management

Easily allocate, swap, and track rooms. View real-time occupancy and manage room types and amenities.

💳

Fee Collection

Track payments, generate invoices, send automated reminders, and view financial reports effortlessly.

🛠️

Complaint System

Students submit maintenance or other complaints which admins can track, assign, and resolve in real-time.

📢

Notice Board

Pin announcements, circulars, and event notices. Students get instant notifications for important updates.

📊

Admin Analytics

Comprehensive dashboards with occupancy trends, revenue charts, complaint resolution rates, and more.

🔒

Secure & Private

Role-based access ensures students only see their data while admins can manage the full system securely.
